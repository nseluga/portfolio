Research · Apr to May 2025

NBA Shot Value

A model of how good an NBA shot is before you know who took it.

  • Uses 128,069 shots from the 2014-15 season, described only by context such as shot distance, defender distance, dribbles, touch time and shot clock.
  • Logistic regression, a decision tree, a random forest and XGBoost all stop at an AUC of 0.63 to 0.64. What is left is shooter skill, which we left out on purpose.
  • SHAP ranks defender distance ahead of shot distance and touch time.
  • An open shot at the rim is worth about 1.85 points, while a contested one from 20 to 22 feet is worth about 0.62.

Batting Average Ability

How much of a batting average is the hitter, and how much is luck?

  • A two-pass mixed-effects model with a random intercept for each player, fitting BABIP first and then batting average.
  • Fit on 4,374 player-seasons from 1,173 hitters between 2015 and 2024, using FanGraphs data.
  • 24.7% of the variation in batting average is stable player skill. On the test set the model explains 45.7% of the variance with a mean error of .0215.
  • Sprint speed is the strongest driver of BABIP after batted-ball mix.

Pitcher Injury Risk

Models of how likely an MLB pitcher is to get hurt, how badly and how soon.

  • Built on 205,911 pitcher-game rows for 3,249 pitchers from 2015 to 2024, using Statcast and injured-list records.
  • Classifiers and survival models combine into one score, Injury Risk+, indexed to 100.
  • Trained on 2015 to 2022 and tested on 2023 and 2024, with walk-forward validation and isotonic calibration.
  • AUC is about 0.57, which seems to be close to what public data can tell you. The clearest signal is a velocity drop of more than 2 mph, which doubles the 30-day injury rate from 6% to 12%.

Hitter Embedding

A neural network that learns a 64-number profile of each MLB hitter from the pitches they see and uses it to project wOBA.

  • The network has a head for each step of a plate appearance (swing, contact, foul or in play, exit velocity, launch angle and spray), and a Markov chain over the count turns those into projected wOBA.
  • Trained on about 6.5 million pitches from 1,672 hitters between 2015 and 2024, with the 2025 season sealed for a pre-registered test.
  • It did not pass that test. RMSE was 0.0452 against 0.0453 for gradient boosting, although rank correlation was 0.47 against 0.42.
  • On 2024 it recovers 60% of the rank-order platoon signal, and swing and contact signals stabilize several times faster than wOBA.

Stacker Bot

Reinforcement learning for Stacker, a Big Two-style card game for 3 to 8 players and 1 or 2 decks.

  • The plan is a Deep Monte-Carlo specialist for 4 players and one deck, and a transformer generalist across all six configurations.
  • The evaluation rules were set before any training, including sealed deals, fixed test seeds, paired bootstrap confidence intervals and pre-registration.
  • The heuristic baseline beats greedy play by 0.656 points per hand over 150 sealed deals.
  • There are no trained-agent results yet. The simulator and evaluation code have 522 tests.
